## Update contract

`turnkey-symfony-update --check` reports the installed FrameworkBundle version and source commit, then discovers the current official 7.4 LTS patch and resolves its official split-package tag. `--apply --dry-run` exposes the exact version, tag, and commit that apply will use. Root-only `--apply` binds Composer to that version, verifies the installed lock reference, refreshes the source record, and rebuilds the production cache.

Major-line upgrades remain a supervised application migration. The v19 updater intentionally follows only the maintained Symfony 7.4 LTS patch channel.

## Deferred issues

- MEDIUM: The sample is intentionally a one-route framework and database smoke application. Broader CRUD scaffolding and browser fixtures are outside the main-flow acceptance contract.
- MEDIUM: Automated major-line Symfony migrations are deferred because they can require application code and recipe changes. The included updater is scoped to 7.4 LTS patches.

## Loop accounting

Product loops begin at zero. Harness, evidence, and test-script corrections do not consume product loops. Only a genuine HIGH or normal-flow product defect found during acceptance consumes the six-loop ceiling.
